Project Overview

I contributed to the collaborative project titled “Impact of Cover Crops on Plant Yield, Insect Pests, Weeds, and Soil Health in Organic Vegetable and Rice Production in Nepal.” I was responsible for agronomic data collection, soil evaluations, growth measurements, and documenting crop performance under different cover crop treatments.

Evaluate the role of cover crops in improving soil fertility and structure
Reduce weed pressure and pest incidence naturally
Enhance crop yield and resilience to heat stress
Promote organic and sustainable production systems in Nepal

Vegetable Trials

Evaluated cover crop impact on cabbage yield and vigor

Soil Evaluation

Recorded soil temperature and monitored nutrient improvement

Field Documentation

Growth assessments, sampling & trial record maintenance

Key Findings

  • 24% higher cabbage yield under cover crop treatments
  • Mung bean increased soil nitrogen & organic matter
  • Cooler soil conditions improved heat resilience
  • Sesbania & mung bean improved rice productivity
  • Sesame & cowpea contributed to soil fertility enrichment

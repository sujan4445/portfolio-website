Research Practice & Symposium

This research was conducted as part of the Research, Practices & Seminar (RPS) platform at IAAS Lamjung Campus, encouraging students to transform classroom knowledge into applied research.

The RPS symposium emphasizes “learning by doing”, offering mentorship and practical guidance to address real agricultural challenges.

Key thematic areas of research included:

  • Agronomy and Plant Breeding
  • Animal Science and Aquaculture
  • Plant Protection
  • Soil Science and Horticulture
  • Social Science and Agro-Ecology

Botanical Control for Citrus Green Mould

This study explores botanical extracts as an eco-friendly alternative to synthetic fungicides for controlling green mould in mandarin citrus caused by Penicillium digitatum.

Chilli and ginger extracts at 15% concentration significantly reduced disease incidence, lowering Percent Disease Index (PDI) and disease severity (%), demonstrating practical post-harvest applications.

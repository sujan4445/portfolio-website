Lamjung Campus – Research, Practices & Seminar (RPS)

The 12th RPS Symposium at IAAS Lamjung Campus provided an invaluable platform for undergraduate researchers to transform classroom knowledge into practical application. Supported by the Research Development-Training and Extension Center (RD-TEC), the event emphasized the principle of “Learning by Doing”.

Key thematic areas of research included:

  • Agronomy and Plant Breeding
  • Animal Science and Aquaculture
  • Plant Protection
  • Soil Science and Horticulture
  • Social Science and Agro-Ecology

Sujan Chapagain – Research Presenter

I am Sujan Chapagain, an undergraduate researcher at IAAS Lamjung Campus. At the 12th RPS Symposium, I presented my study on the Diversity assessment and agro-morphological characterization of Nepalese finger millet landraces.

My research highlighted the genetic variability of local landraces and emphasized their role in strengthening food security, promoting climate resilience, and conserving agrobiodiversity.

Best Presenter Award

For my presentation and research contribution, I was honored with the Best Presenter Award by the Department of Agronomy and Plant Breeding. This recognition reflects the importance of promoting indigenous crops like finger millet in modern agricultural research.

Sujan Chapagain

Researcher. Author. Board Advisor.

About

I am a confident and dedicated undergraduate student pursuing a B.Sc. in Agriculture at IAAS, TU, with a strong foundation in sustainable farming and crop research. My research work focuses on crop characterization, abiotic stress tolerance, and nutrient management to support resilient agriculture.

My scholarship sits at the intersection of sustainable agriculture, climate resilience, and food security. Broadly, my work explores how modern agricultural practices can be optimized for various environmental conditions, by asking timely questions that investigate important topics such as crop adaptation strategies, soil health management, and sustainable farming techniques.

I believe in teamwork, stress management, and integrity while taking on leadership roles and driving youth-led initiatives. With solid technical knowledge and strong communication skills, my goal is to grow in a way that benefits both my career and the communities I serve.

In 2024, I was recognized for my contributions to agricultural research and sustainable farming practices. I continue to work on projects that bridge the gap between traditional farming methods and modern agricultural innovations.

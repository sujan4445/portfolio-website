Tenure & Position

National Campaign Ambassador Jan 2024 – Jan 2025

Roles, Activities & Contributions

As the National Campaign Ambassador of IAAS Nepal, I worked to bridge the gap between national-level initiatives and local committee efforts. My role focused on strengthening campaign outreach, creating awareness on sustainable agriculture, and engaging youth in climate justice and food security. I coordinated with local committees across Nepal, ensuring better visibility and impact of IAAS campaigns while promoting the voices of students and farmers at both the grassroots and national platforms.

Representation & Major Events

Some of the major contributions and representation I undertook during my tenure include:

  4. Represented IAAS Nepal in dialogues with stakeholders, amplifying youth perspectives in agriculture policy discussions.
  5. Enhanced visibility of IAAS campaigns through creative communication strategies and national coordination.

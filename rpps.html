Research Practice & Symposium

This study was part of the Research, Practices & Seminar (RPS) platform at IAAS Lamjung Campus, where students apply scientific research methodology to real-world challenges.

The symposium emphasizes learning by doing, fostering mentorship, analytical thinking and professional scientific communication.

Core thematic categories:

  • Agronomy and Plant Breeding
  • Animal Science and Aquaculture
  • Plant Protection
  • Soil Science and Horticulture
  • Social Science and Agro-Ecology

Sujan Chapagain – Research Presenter

I presented my research entitled Diversity assessment and agro-morphological characterization of Nepalese finger millet landraces focusing on conserving indigenous crop resources and improving climate resilience.

The study provides valuable insights for future breeding programs and agro-biodiversity conservation initiatives.

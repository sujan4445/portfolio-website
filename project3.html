Morpho-Physiological Screening of Wheat under Drought

Evaluated drought tolerance of wheat genotypes using RWC, leaf rolling, and yield stability

Description

Frequent terminal droughts constrain wheat productivity in mid-hills. This project screened genotypes under irrigated and rainfed conditions to identify drought-tolerant lines. Traits such as relative water content (RWC), leaf rolling index, plant height, harvest index, and grain yield were measured, and multivariate analyses including AMMI, GGE, and cluster analysis were conducted to identify stable and superior genotypes.

Background

This study addresses the yield losses in wheat due to drought by evaluating physiological and morpho-agronomic traits under controlled water stress.

Objectives

  • Screen wheat genotypes for drought tolerance.
  • Identify stable and high-yielding lines under drought conditions.
  • Support breeding programs for stress-resilient wheat varieties.

Methods

Field trials under irrigated and rainfed conditions; measurements included RWC, leaf rolling, plant height, HI, and grain yield. Data analyzed using AMMI, GGE biplot, and cluster analysis.

Funding & Timeline

Funded by departmental support; conducted from March 2025 to February 2026.

Research Practices and Seminar (RPS) Symposium

The Research Practices and Seminar (RPS) Symposium serves as an academic platform where students, researchers, and professionals share innovative research ideas, discuss methodologies, and enhance research practices. It fosters critical thinking, academic collaboration, and encourages young scholars to present and defend their research work in a professional setting.

Food and Youth Institute (FAYI) International Symposium

The FAYI International Symposium is a global platform that brings together researchers, young professionals, and policymakers to discuss pressing issues in food security, youth engagement in agriculture, and sustainable development. It emphasizes collaborative approaches, research innovation, and youth-led solutions to tackle modern agricultural challenges.

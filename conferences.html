Food and Youth Institute (FAYI) Symposium

August 2023 — Youth with Connected Minds in Agriculture & Food

The Food and Youth Institute (FAYI) International Symposium held in August 2023 brought together passionate young researchers, students, and innovators working in agriculture and food systems. With the theme "Youth Empowerment for Sustainable Futures: A Symposium on Agriculture, Food Security, and One Health", the symposium created a platform to share ideas, showcase research, and promote collaborations for sustainable agriculture and resilient food systems.

The event highlighted the role of youth in advancing agricultural research, climate-smart practices, and food security strategies. Participants engaged in discussions on crop innovation, nutrition-sensitive farming, policy advocacy, and technological applications for rural development. The symposium was not only an academic gathering but also a celebration of collective action for a sustainable future.

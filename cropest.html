Academic & Trainings

Highlights from workshops, seminars, and field trainings

Crop Pest Diagnosis

Learned to identify major crop pests, recognize symptoms on different crops, and use diagnostic tools for pest detection.

Crop Pest Management

Gained hands-on experience in integrated pest management techniques including biological control, chemical safety, and crop protection planning.

Surveillance & Pest Status Determination

Learned to monitor pest populations, assess outbreak risks, and record regional pest incidence for informed decision-making.

Emergency Preparedness for Pest Outbreaks

I gained knowledge in developing and implementing strategic frameworks for the rapid detection, assessment, and management of pest outbreaks.

Phytosanitary Capacity Evaluation

Evaluated national and institutional capacities for plant health compliance, quarantine procedures, and international trade standards.

Mobilizing Resources for Plant Health

Learned strategies to plan, allocate, and mobilize financial and human resources effectively for plant health programs.

Sustainable Agriculture

Gained knowledge on sustainable farming practices, eco-friendly techniques, and ways to enhance soil fertility and crop yield without harming the environment.

Good Practice to Cope with Soil

Learned practical techniques for soil conservation, erosion prevention, and improving soil health through organic and mechanical methods.

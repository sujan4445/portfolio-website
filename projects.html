Research Projects

Exploring crop resilience, plant pathology, and agricultural phenotyping through empirical field trials and data science.

01 Plant Physiology & Phenotyping

Morpho-Physiological & Performance Evaluation of Wheat Genotypes Under Drought Stress

  • Focus: Bread Wheat (Triticum aestivum) Resilience
  • Integrated spectral reflectance (NDVI), canopy temperature depression, and root architecture modeling to evaluate high-yielding wheat genotypes under water-deficit conditions.
02 Crop Protection & Pathology

In vivo Efficacy of Botanical Extracts Against Penicillium digitatum Causing Green Mould in Citrus

  • Focus: Post-Harvest Disease Management
  • Evaluated bio-degradable organic extracts (capsaicinoids & allicin) as sustainable, low-cost antifungal alternatives to synthetic chemical fungicides in mandarin citrus.
03 Biodiversity & Genetic Resources

Agro-Morphological Diversity Assessment in Nepalese Finger Millet Landraces

  • Focus: Climate-Resilient Nepalese Landraces (Eleusine coracana)
  • Characterized phenotypic variation across indigenous landraces to pinpoint high-yielding, stress-tolerant lines suitable for climate adaptation and plant breeding.

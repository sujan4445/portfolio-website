Agro-Morphological Diversity in Finger Millet

Nepal harbors significant finger millet landrace diversity adapted to hilly and mountain agro-ecologies. This project characterizes landraces for genetic, phenotypic, and agronomic variability to inform selection and on-farm conservation.

Background

Finger millet (Eleusine coracana) remains a vital staple for mid-hills households. Landraces show wide variability for yield, maturity, and stress tolerance, yet are under-documented.

Place

IAAS, Sundarbazar, Lamjung, Nepal

Objectives

  • Quantify phenotypic diversity for key agro-morphological traits.
  • Estimate heritability and genetic advance.
  • Identify divergent clusters for breeding and conservation.

Design / Method

Alpha lattice RCBD (2 reps); traits: PH, DM, EL, TGW, GY, etc.; ANOVA, PCA, clustering, and variability parameters (H2, GA%).

Funding

Internal (IAAS mini-grant) — ₹ / NPR amount

Background

Frequent terminal droughts constrain wheat productivity in mid-hills. Identifying drought-tolerant genotypes supports resilient farming systems.

Place

IAAS, Sundarbazar, Lamjung, Nepal

Objectives

  • Screen genotypes under contrasting moisture regimes.
  • Estimate heritability of morpho-physiological traits.
  • Classify drought response via multivariate analysis.

Design / Method

Split-plot with irrigation as main plot; traits: RWC, LRI, PH, HI, GY; stability via AMMI/GGE; clustering & PCA.

Background

Early seedling traits are crucial for drought escape and adaptation. Identifying seedling-stage tolerant genotypes accelerates breeding for resilience in water-limited environments.

Place

IAAS, Sundarbazar, Lamjung, Nepal

Objectives

  • Evaluate 20 wheat genotypes under different field capacity levels.
  • Characterize early root and shoot traits linked to drought tolerance.
  • Identify promising genotypes for breeding and field testing.

Design / Method

Pot experiment with three moisture regimes (100%, 75%, 50% FC); traits: root length, root-to-shoot ratio, seedling dry weight, vigor index; analysis via ANOVA, PCA & clustering.

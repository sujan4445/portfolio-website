Research Projects

Documenting diversity, resilience, and performance of crops in Nepal

Agro-Morphological Diversity in Finger Millet

This project explores Nepal’s rich finger millet landrace diversity. It identifies traits such as seed size, panicle structure, and stress tolerance, guiding breeding programs for climate-resilient crops.

Conservation strategies derived from this research help preserve genetic diversity and promote sustainable farming systems.

Morpho-Physiological Screening of Wheat under Drought

This project evaluates phenotypic traits, root architecture, and yield of wheat under drought stress. Combined with genotypic data, it helps identify drought-tolerant lines for breeding resilient wheat varieties in Nepal.

It informs sustainable crop management and climate-adaptive strategies to improve productivity under water-limited conditions.

More Details

Performance Evaluation of Wheat Genotypes under Irrigated and Drought Conditions

This research compares wheat genotypes under irrigated and drought environments, evaluating growth, yield, and stress adaptation to select high-performance varieties for variable climates.

It guides targeted breeding programs, optimizing genotype selection for resilience, yield stability, and nutritional quality.

More Details

Screening Wheat Genotypes for Drought Tolerance via Early Seedling Traits

Early seedling traits provide a rapid indicator of drought tolerance. This project evaluates germination, seedling vigor, and root development to identify resilient genotypes.

Findings support sustainable agroecological intensification, soil health improvement, and biodiversity-friendly cropping systems.

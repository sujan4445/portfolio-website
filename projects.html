Research Projects

Documenting diversity, resilience, and performance of crops in Nepal

Project 1
Details

Agro-Morphological Diversity in Finger Millet

This project explores Nepal's rich finger millet landrace diversity. It identifies traits such as seed size, panicle structure, and stress tolerance, guiding breeding programs for climate-resilient crops.
Project 2
Details

Morpho-Physiological Screening of Wheat under Drought

This project evaluates phenotypic traits, root architecture, and yield of wheat under drought stress. Combined with genotypic data, it helps identify drought-tolerant lines for breeding resilient wheat varieties.
Project 3
Details

Performance Evaluation of Wheat Genotypes

This research compares wheat genotypes under irrigated and drought environments, evaluating growth, yield, and stress adaptation to select high-performance varieties for variable climates.
Project 4
Details

Screening Wheat Genotypes for Drought Tolerance

Early seedling traits provide a rapid indicator of drought tolerance. This project evaluates germination, seedling vigor, and root development to identify resilient genotypes.

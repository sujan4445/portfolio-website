Research Projects

Exploring plant health, Crop protection, Botanical control,and crop resilience for sustainable agriculture

Project 1
Details

In vivo Efficacy of Botanical Extracts against Penicillium digitatum (Pers.: Fr.) Sacc.

Investigated the antifungal potential of botanical extracts against green mould in citrus. The study demonstrated significant inhibitory effects, highlighting eco-friendly alternatives to synthetic fungicides for postharvest disease control.
Project 2
Details

Phenotypic Screening & Diversity Analysis of Wheat Genotypes against Leaf Rust (Puccinia triticina)

Evaluated wheat genotypes for resistance against leaf rust, assessing phenotypic variability and genetic diversity to identify resistant lines for sustainable wheat breeding programs.
Project 3
Details

Effect of Botanical Treatments and Oxalic Acid on Shelf Life and Quality of Mandarin

Assessed the role of plant-based treatments and oxalic acid in reducing postharvest decay. Treatments with Aloe vera and garlic extracts minimized spoilage and maintained fruit quality during storage.
Project 4
Details

Cultural Characterization of Alternaria brassicicola (Schw.) Wilts Infecting Cabbage

Characterized colony morphology and sporulation on different media to identify the optimal growth conditions of A. brassicicola. Findings aid in pathogen isolation and integrated disease management strategies.
Project 5
Details

Infestation Status of Stink Bug and Its Impact on Mandarin Farmers: A Case of Tanahu, Nepal

Surveyed citrus orchards to assess stink bug infestation levels and associated economic losses. Results revealed significant yield reduction, emphasizing the need for integrated pest management in mandarin cultivation.

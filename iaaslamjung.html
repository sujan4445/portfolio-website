Roles, Activities & Contributions

As part of IAAS LC Lamjung, I have been actively involved in strengthening youth engagement in agriculture and sustainable development. My journey includes leading local committee strategies, supporting exchange programs, and fostering cross-cultural learning among agricultural students.

During my tenure as Local Director, I coordinated campaigns, exchange opportunities, and workshops to connect students with practical agricultural innovation and global experiences. This helped build a platform for Lamjung students to showcase local agricultural practices internationally.

As Quality Board member, I worked on improving the exchange department’s efficiency, ensuring transparent processes for both incoming and outgoing students, and creating meaningful experiences that combined academic and cultural exposure.

Overall, my work with IAAS LC Lamjung emphasized leadership, networking, and the promotion of sustainable agriculture among youth at both local and global levels.

Ankur Poudel

Ph.D., Assistant Professor / Mentor
Undergraduate Research and Thesis Supervisor

Dr. Ankur Poudel is an expert in plant stress biology with a strong focus on genomics and molecular approaches. His work integrates advanced genomic tools to understand crop adaptation under abiotic stress, with a vision to improve sustainable agriculture. He also mentors undergraduate students in research and thesis projects.

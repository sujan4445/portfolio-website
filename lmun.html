Model United Nation (MUN) is the simulation of one of the assemblies of the United Nations. It aims at providing youths and students with deep insights into global diplomacy, negotiation, and international relations. MUN activities include debating, public speaking, research, and writing, where participants act as delegates representing assigned countries.

MUN is about learning the world and addressing global issues in a spirit of fun and teamwork, while building leadership skills.

Youth Thinkers’ Society – Lamjung fosters this platform for young leaders to engage in debate, diplomacy, and problem-solving. This initiative reflects YTS-Lamjung’s unwavering commitment to youth engagement, advancement, and igniting the power of the next generation for a sustainable future.

The Lamjung Model United Nations (LMUN) has been a landmark youth initiative in Lamjung, creating a platform for aspiring diplomats, debaters, and change-makers. Since its inception, LMUN has grown into one of the most awaited intellectual events, drawing participation from schools, colleges, and youth organizations.

Over the years, the conference has provided participants with hands-on experience in policy-making, negotiations, leadership, and global problem-solving. Each edition carried unique themes reflecting pressing global issues — from climate change to human rights, from peacebuilding to sustainable development.

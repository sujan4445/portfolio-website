Project Overview

I contributed to the collaborative research project titled “Impact of Cover Crops on Yield, Insect Pests, Weeds, and Soil Health in Organic Vegetable and Rice Production in Nepal.” In my role as Field Data Collector & Research Support, I was responsible for agronomic data collection, soil evaluations, growth measurements, and documenting crop performance under various cover crop treatments.

Key Project Objectives

  • Evaluate the role of cover crops in improving soil fertility and structure
  • Reduce weed pressure and pest incidence through natural ecological management
  • Enhance crop yield and resilience to environmental heat stress
  • Promote organic and sustainable agricultural production systems across Nepal

Field Work & Scope

Vegetable Trials

Evaluated cover crop impact on cabbage yield, overall plant vigor, and agro-morphological parameters under organic field management.

Soil Evaluation

Monitored and recorded soil temperatures, assessed soil organic matter, and tracked nutrient dynamics across treatments.

Field Documentation

Conducted systematic field sampling, growth assessments, and rigorous trial record maintenance throughout the two-year trial period.

Key Findings

  • Observed a 24% higher cabbage yield under cover crop treatments compared to control plots
  • Mung bean integration noticeably increased soil nitrogen content and soil organic matter
  • Cooler micro-soil conditions enhanced crop heat resilience during peak warm periods
  • Sesbania and mung bean significantly enhanced rice productivity in rotational setups
  • Sesame and cowpea cover crops contributed substantially to long-term soil fertility enrichment

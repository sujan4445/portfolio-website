Research Profile

I am an agriculture graduate with a strong research focus on plant pathology, sustainable disease management, and crop health assessment. My academic and research training integrates fungal pathogen biology, botanical-based disease control, and field-level stress phenotyping.

I have hands-on experience in pathogen isolation, cultural and morphological characterization of phytopathogenic fungi, phytochemical extraction, and in vivo evaluation of botanical extracts against postharvest and field diseases.

My research work emphasizes eco-friendly alternatives to synthetic pesticides, with particular attention to the physiological and biochemical responses of crops to biotic and abiotic stresses. In field-based studies, I have applied NDVI and canopy temperature measurements to quantify plant vigor, disease severity, and stress tolerance in wheat genotypes.

I am proficient in quantitative data analysis using R, experimental design, and scientific writing, with peer-reviewed publications in crop improvement, disease management, and agronomy.

My long-term academic goal is to advance expertise in plant health, molecular diagnostics, and integrated disease management to contribute to sustainable and climate-resilient agricultural systems.

Research Profile & Background

Sujan Chapagain is an agricultural researcher dedicated to cereal plant pathology, crop stress physiology, and host resistance genetics. His core research interest is on genetic improvement and agronomic management of spike and leaf related disease in small grains such as Fusarium Head Blight, Stripe Rust, and Leaf rust, featuring hands-on expertise in Greenhouse spore inoculation techniques, field based phenotyping screneing and nursery management and high-throughput phenotyping and index scoring. His academic background encompasses abiotic and biotic stress screening, postharvest disease management, and crop genetic diversity analysis. His technical toolkit covers field phenotyping (NDVI, canopy temperature), molecular diagnostics (DNA extraction, PCR assays, marker design), experimental design, spatial/statistical analysis (R, Python, GIS), and scientific writing. Sujan's ultimate objective is to advance climate-resilient agriculture through breeding for disease resistance and molecular plant-pathogen interaction studies.
